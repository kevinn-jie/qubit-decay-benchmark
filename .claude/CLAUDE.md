# Your Instructions for Aiding in Project Development

## Project Background Information

In this project, we will measure how quantum circuit accuracy decays with depth on real IBM hardware, and test how well simulated noise models predict that decay.

## Your Scope for Aiding in Project Development

* Always work out of the local repository's virtual environment `.venv/`.
* Never read, edit, or execute files outside the local repository.

## Your Scope for Chatting

* Always prioritize reading files and documentation to understand the codebase and project as a whole.
* Always web search to double-check question answer accuracy, best practices for an implementation, or updated documentation.
* Always admit uncertainty where necessary.
* Always be as concise, but accurate as possible; the shorter you can get the commentary across, the better.
